Cymatics Lab

Real-time Chladni-style plate simulation driven by oscillator, microphone, or audio file.
OSCILLATOR · 180 Hz
For a physical cymatics plate, use the displayed frequency as the excitation target. This browser simulation visualizes the corresponding resonant-mode pattern; it does not physically vibrate your plate.
How to use: Start the audio engine, choose Oscillator for a controlled frequency, or choose Microphone / Audio file to make the visual respond to sound. For speech, select Microphone and speak normally. The pattern continuously estimates the dominant frequency and maps it to a resonant mode.
